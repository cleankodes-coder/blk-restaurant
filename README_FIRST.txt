WHAT IS INSIDE

- menu-reference.json: 58 item names transcribed from provided historical menu references. Prices are intentionally absent because screenshots date from Jan 2025.

QUALITY NOTES
- Hero photograph is portrait, but high-resolution (3072 x 4080): use a split hero to avoid heavy crops.
- Food and exterior/ambience photos are authentic, but most are 320–640px; use at restrained sizes.
- BLK logo source originally had a white background. Transparent PNGs are isolated from that original mark; no newly invented logo.
- An up-to-date official price/menu and wide interior image were not provided; avoid inventing them.
- Copyright/usage permissions for downloaded social images should be confirmed before public commercial use.
- Location details, reservation provider, hours and phone must be confirmed with the restaurant before production launch.
